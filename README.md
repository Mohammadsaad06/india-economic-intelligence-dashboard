# 🇮🇳 India Economic Intelligence Dashboard

### An Interactive Power BI Analysis of India's Economy

## 📌 Project Overview

The **India Economic Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a comprehensive view of India's economic performance through key macroeconomic indicators, government finances, trade, banking, sectoral performance, state-wise GSDP, foreign investment and taxation.

The dashboard transforms multiple government and RBI datasets into meaningful visual insights to help understand India's economic trends and performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse India's major economic indicators.
- Track GDP, inflation, industrial production and unemployment.
- Understand India's export, import and trade balance trends.
- Monitor government receipts, expenditure and fiscal deficits.
- Analyse banking credit and sector-wise distribution.
- Study GVA and state-wise GSDP performance.
- Examine foreign investment and government tax revenue.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

| No. | Dashboard Page | Key Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Economic Overview | IIP, CPI, inflation, unemployment, repo rate and USD/INR |
| 02 | GDP & Economic Indicators | Real GDP, nominal GDP and growth comparison |
| 03 | Trade & Economic Insights | Exports, imports, trade balance and commodities |
| 04 | Fiscal & Government Finance | Government receipts, expenditure, deficits and liabilities |
| 05 | Banking & Credit Indicators | Bank credit trends and sectoral distribution |
| 06 | Sectoral Economic Performance | GVA trends and industry-wise contribution |
| 07 | State-wise Economic Performance | State-wise GSDP and growth comparisons |
| 08 | Investment & Taxation | FDI, FPI and government tax revenue |
